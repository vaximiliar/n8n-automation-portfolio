# Lead Capture & Auto Email

## Overview

This workflow automatically captures new leads from a Google Form, stores them in Google Sheets, sends a confirmation email to the customer, notifies the manager, and updates the lead status.

## Features

- Capture leads from Google Forms
- Store data in Google Sheets
- Send confirmation email
- Notify manager
- Update lead status automatically

## Tech Stack

- n8n
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Gmail

## Workflow

Google Form → Google Sheets → Gmail → Google Sheets
