# n8n Automation Portfolio

A collection of business workflow automations built with **n8n**.

## Projects

### 1. Lead Capture & Auto Email
Automatically captures leads from a Google Form, stores them in Google Sheets, sends a confirmation email to the customer, notifies the manager, and updates the lead status.

**Tech Stack**
- n8n
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Gmail

---

### 2. Lead Qualification
Automatically analyzes incoming leads and classifies them as **Hot**, **Warm**, or **Cold** based on message content.

**Tech Stack**
- n8n
- JavaScript
- Google Sheets

---

### 3. Appointment Booking
Automatically schedules meetings in Google Calendar, sends a confirmation email, and updates the booking status.

**Tech Stack**
- n8n
- Google Calendar
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets
- Gmail
